FAQ

Everything you need to know

Can't find your answer? Chat with our team

What platforms does PulseAI support?
PulseAI supports Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, X (Twitter), and Threads. We're constantly adding new platforms based on customer feedback. You can manage all of them from one unified dashboard without switching between apps.
How does the AI content generation work?
Our AI learns your brand voice, audience, and top-performing content to generate posts that feel authentically yours. Simply describe what you want to post about, choose a tone, and the AI creates captions, hashtags, and even suggests the best posting times — all in seconds.
Can I try PulseAI before paying?
Absolutely. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to all features — no credit card required. If you decide PulseAI isn't right for you, simply cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged a thing.
How many team members can I add?
The Starter plan is designed for solo use. Professional includes 5 team seats with role-based permissions (Owner, Editor, Viewer). The Agency plan includes unlimited seats, making it perfect for teams managing multiple client accounts with approval workflows.
Is my data secure with PulseAI?
Security is a top priority. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We never sell your data, and we only request the minimum permissions needed to schedule and publish your content. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR-ready.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?
Yes, completely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time directly from your account settings — no phone calls, no hassle. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period. We'll even send you a reminder before your trial ends.
